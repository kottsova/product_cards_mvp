Tender, moist, crisp and more even: Steam means better baking
The 500 SurroundCook Oven with SteamBake  lets you add a bit of steam for 
just the right colour and crust. Dishes are golden-crispy outside, tender and 
moist inside. And every dish is evenly cooked without having to turn or move 
the tray.

More even heat throughout the oven with Multilevel 
Cooking
Multilevel Cooking adds third heating element to enable 
you to bake evenly on multiple levels. Perfect for 
creating three trays of cookies or pies at the same time.
The Food Sensor helps you cook with confidence
The Food Sensor helps you create delicious meals 
every time by continuously measuring the temperature 
in the center of your food. When it reaches the right 
temperature, the oven lets you know and stops cooking.
For delicious baking, just add steam
The SteamBake multifunction oven uses steam for 
delicious baking. Dishes are golden-crispy outside 
while the inside remains tender and moist. Baking with 
steam also gives you food that is evenly cooked in 
every corner.
The SteamBake oven bakes with steam so dishes are crispy outside, tender and moist inside.
Catalytic Cleaning – cleans the oven for you
The Catalytic Cleaning feature in your freestanding 
oven helps to avoid the build-up of dirt and grease. The 
self-cleaning technology automatically starts to work 
when the oven's temperature reaches 250°C, making 
cleaning effortless.
EXPlore LED Display with Touch buttons
The EXPlore display allows you to effortlessly control 
the settings of your oven to know what’s happening with 
your cooking. With the LED display and 5-touch 
buttons, you can easily see the time, temperature and 
progress of your dish.
