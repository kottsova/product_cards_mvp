Suggestions for a correct use in order to reduce the environmental impact: 
• Make sure that the oven door is closed properly when the oven operates. Do not open the door too often 
during cooking. Keep the door gasket clean and make sure it is well fixed in its position.
• Use metal cookware to improve energy saving.
• When possible, do not preheat the oven before cooking.
• Keep breaks between baking as short as possible when you prepare a few dishes at one time.
• Other information available on chapter “Energy Efficiency” of User Manual
