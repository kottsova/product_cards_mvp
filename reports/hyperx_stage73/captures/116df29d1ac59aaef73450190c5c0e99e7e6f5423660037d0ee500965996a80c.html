    Can I use the Orbit S with an amp?

    It is not recommended to use an amp with the Orbit S headset because the standard line level voltage must not be exceeded. If the amp outputs more than the line level, the headset may experience distortion or damage.

    Will the HyperX CloudX Stinger Core Wireless turn off automatically when the console is powered off?

    Yes, like the Xbox controller, the headset will turn off automatically if the console is powered off. Conversely, powering on the headset will power on the console from standby mode.
