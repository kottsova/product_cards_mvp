    HyperX Pulsefire Haste 3 Pro Wireless Charging

    Connect the mouse to a PC or game console using the included USB cable. When the battery level drops below 10%, the mouse wheel blinks red. When it's less than 5%, the LED turns off. *USB connectivity will override the selected power mode.
