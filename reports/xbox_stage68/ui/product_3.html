Возможности HDMI: Auto Low Latency Mode. HDMI Variable Refresh Rate. AMD FreeSync.. Аудиоформаты: Dolby Digital 5.1; DTS 5.1; Dolby TrueHD with Atmos; Up to 7.1 L-PCM. HDMI: 1x HDMI 2.1 port. USB-порты: 3x USB 3.1 Gen 1 ports. Стандарт Wi-Fi: 802.11ac dual band. Ethernet: 802.3 10/100/1000. Xbox Wireless: Dedicated dual band XBOX Wireless radio.
