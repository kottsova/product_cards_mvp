    Performance

    Welcome to the mother chip.

    A20 Pro is our most powerful and efficient iPhone chip design ever, with packaging inspired by our M-series chips. Working with the next‑generation vapor chamber, it gives you the best possible graphics performance — up to 40 percent faster than iPhone 17 Pro. And introduces a new Dual 16‑core Neural Engine with two times the compute for on‑device intelligence.5

    Exceptional
    AI capabilities

    More power for all your AI tasks. And faster Apple Intelligence thanks to new Neural Accelerators, a Dual 16‑core Neural Engine, and 50 percent higher memory bandwidth.

    M‑class-inspired
    packaging design

    By placing the silicon die side by side with memory, the A20 Pro chip packaging drastically improves heat flow for significantly better thermal performance.

    Bigger vapor chamber.
    Let the heat drop.

    The next-generation vapor chamber has three times the surface area to dissipate even more heat. Combined with more thermally conductive materials, it works with the A20 Pro chip design to deliver up to 40 percent better sustained performance than iPhone 17 Pro. So you can push high-intensity gaming and run larger, more complex AI models on your device for longer.
